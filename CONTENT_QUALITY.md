# Brali Growth Library content quality

The Growth Library is product content, not an SEO page factory. Public guidance should be useful when read by a person and defensible when read by a search engine, reviewer, or AI system.

## Evidence states

Every entry should move toward one of these explicit states:

- `reviewed`: evidence-like claims have traceable sources and the practical guidance has been checked.
- `practical`: the entry is framed as a low-risk personal practice and avoids scientific or clinical claims it cannot support.
- `pending-review`: useful source material exists, but evidence and wording still need review.
- `restricted`: sensitive health or mental-health guidance without adequate sourcing. It should not be indexed until reviewed.

## Claims

Precise claims require precise support. Percentages, sample sizes, effect estimates, phrases such as "research shows", and claims about treatment, diagnosis, prevention, or clinical outcomes require a source that a reader can trace.

When a source is unavailable, prefer a modest practical statement over scientific-sounding language. Do not invent a study-shaped explanation to make a recommendation look authoritative.

## Health and mental health

Health and mental-health entries receive the highest review priority.

Until an entry has adequate sources and review:

- keep it out of search indexing;
- label it as pending evidence review;
- frame it as general education or reflection rather than medical advice;
- avoid diagnosis, treatment promises, or claims that an exercise can replace professional care.

## Structure of a strong protocol

A strong Brali entry should answer:

1. What problem is this for?
2. What should I try?
3. How small can the first action be?
4. How long should I try it before reviewing?
5. What should I observe or record?
6. What would make me keep, change, or stop it?
7. What evidence or source supports any factual claim?
8. Are there safety limits or contexts where this is not appropriate?

## Brand and provenance

Public pages use Brali branding. Historical MetalHatsCats URLs and names may remain in archived source records for provenance, but they must not leak into newly generated public pages.

## Search policy

Indexing is earned by content quality, not by the existence of a generated URL.

Do not increase indexed page count simply because the generator can. Prefer a smaller set of distinct, useful, reviewed protocol pages to a large set of repetitive articles.

## Automation

The repository content audit should detect at least:

- legacy branding in generated output;
- sensitive unsourced pages that remain indexable;
- evidence-like claims without explicit sources;
- future evidence/review metadata gaps as the data model evolves.

Blocking checks should focus first on risks that can be identified reliably. Non-blocking warnings form the editorial review queue.
