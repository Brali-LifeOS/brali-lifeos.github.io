# Contributing to Brali

Brali accepts original knowledge and protocol proposals, corrections, source suggestions, taxonomy proposals, research collaboration proposals, and integration/partnership proposals. Pick the matching GitHub issue form so the review path is explicit. The public path from intake to review and publication is documented at `/agents/contribute/`.

## Evidence boundary

A citation, abstract, DOI, search result, or AI summary is not reviewed evidence. Evidence-like submissions must identify the exact source and the claim it is meant to support or challenge. See `SOURCE_POLICY.md` and `CONTENT_QUALITY.md`.

## Contribution paths

- **Knowledge or protocol proposal**: your own practical technique, a short executable sequence, an improvement to an existing protocol, or a problem Brali should cover. A research citation is optional for an original practical proposal; attribution is required when it comes from someone else.
- **Source suggestion**: a paper, guideline, review, replication, negative/null result, or other source worth screening.
- **Correction**: a factual, provenance, wording, broken-link, or data problem in an existing item.
- **Taxonomy proposal**: a Domain/Topic/Method/Lens classification change with affected canonical IDs and legacy URLs.
- **Research collaboration**: a transparent research/editorial project; do not post confidential material.
- **Integration / partnership**: APIs, MCP, localization, commercial licensing, custom collections, or product integration.

Every submission begins as a proposal. Ordinary corrections can be accepted after maintainer review. Evidence-state promotion and sensitive claims require the evidence-review workflow. Commercial reuse remains governed by `LICENSE` and `LICENSING.md`.
