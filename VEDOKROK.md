# Vedokrok — project history

Brali Life OS is now part of [Vedokrok](https://vedokrok.com), a broader practical knowledge system. This document records how the project got here and what the consolidation means for this repository and site.

## The arc: experiment → lessons → consolidation → Vedokrok

### Experiment

Brali Life OS started as a personal organizer. Over time it became a knowledge project built around one observation: knowledge read once and never used has little value. Brali explored a single question — why does useful knowledge so rarely change what people do? — and experimented with turning useful ideas into repeatable actions: short protocols with explicit fit and evidence limits, stable identities, provenance, and machine-readable contracts for both humans and AI agents.

### Lessons

The experiment produced a working model, not just content:

- **Trust state has to travel with the content.** `reviewed`, `practical`, `pending-review`, and `restricted` are operational states that decide what can be recommended, not decorative labels.
- **Bounded recommendations beat open-ended advice.** A protocol with `when`, `why`, and `caveat` logic is more useful than a page of tips.
- **One knowledge model can feed many surfaces.** Human pages, JSON APIs, Agent Skills, MCP, demos, and evaluation artifacts all derive from the same canonical records — and stay honest because of it.

### Consolidation

Those lessons outgrew a single-area project. The model generalizes: every area of practical knowledge has the same gap between reading and doing, and the same need for knowledge that is ready at the moment it can change an action or decision.

### Vedokrok

That consolidation is [Vedokrok](https://vedokrok.com) — a broader practical knowledge system built on the same principle Brali explored. Where Brali was a focused project around one area of practical knowledge, Vedokrok connects thousands of practical ideas, techniques, frameworks and tools across many areas of life and work — with collections, evidence, structured knowledge and future AI access. Active development continues at Vedokrok.

## What this means

- **The site stays up.** `https://brali-lifeos.github.io` remains online as a historical project and a useful standalone resource.
- **No redirects.** Brali URLs keep serving their content; nothing is silently forwarded elsewhere.
- **Content is preserved.** The knowledge model, evidence states, provenance, public APIs, and Agent Skills library remain published and inspectable, and this repository remains the canonical source for them.
