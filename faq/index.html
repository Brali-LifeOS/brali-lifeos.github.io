FAQ

Simple answers about Brali.

Brali started as a large habit tracker and personal organizer. Today the main focus is the library: useful ideas, better evidence, open data, and access for people and AI.

What is Brali today?

Brali is a public library of practical ideas. The ideas are grouped by Life Area, Growth Zone, and protocol. The data can also be used by apps and AI agents.

Why call them protocols instead of hacks?

A hack sounds like a shortcut that should work for everyone. A protocol is simply something to try, plus a way to check whether it helped.

Are all protocols scientifically proven?

No. We show the difference. An item can be reviewed, practical, pending-review, or restricted. We do not want a useful suggestion to pretend it has stronger evidence than it really does.

How should I use a protocol?

Pick one that fits a real problem. Try a small version. Notice what happens. Keep it, change it, or drop it.

Can AI agents use Brali data?

Yes, subject to the license. Start with For AI & developers and the Trusted Protocol Feed.

What is the Trusted Protocol Feed?

It is a smaller JSON feed containing entries that meet Brali's current public quality bar. The full library is broader and includes material that may still need review.

Can I use Brali data commercially?

The public license is non-commercial. Paid products, commercial AI use, and redistribution in commercial services require a separate agreement. See Partnerships.

Can I use Brali for research or a non-commercial project?

Yes, if you follow the repository license. Keep attribution and source links where required.

Is Brali available in multiple languages?

English is the main published language today. More languages are planned. We want translations to stay connected to the same underlying protocol instead of becoming separate copies with no history.

How are weak claims corrected?

We keep an evidence index and review queue. Claims can be corrected, downgraded, or removed from normal discovery when needed.

Is this medical, legal, or financial advice?

No. Brali is general educational and personal-development material. Sensitive topics need a higher review bar.

Is the mobile app still the main product?

No. The app is still available, but the library and data are now the main project.

How can an organization work with Brali?

Possible areas include data licensing, AI integrations, localization, research, and custom collections. See Partnerships.
