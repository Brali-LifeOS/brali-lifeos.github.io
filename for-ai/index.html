Knowledge model

Use Domain → Topic → Hack → Protocol as the primary practical stack. Add a Method when a named structured approach is used, and a Lens when the content borrows a way of thinking from a discipline, profession, philosophy, or strategic tradition. Methods and Lenses are classification dimensions, not substitutes for the user's concrete Topic and not evidence by themselves.

Legacy Life Area and Growth Zone fields remain for compatibility. Use ontology.json to resolve each old Growth Zone to a Topic, Method, or Lens rather than assuming all old zones are the same kind of thing.

If a record has classification_status: topic-pending, do not invent a Topic. The current backlog is published in Ontology Coverage.

For recommending published knowledge

  1. Identify the concrete Topic that matches the user's need.
  2. Retrieve a few protocols from the trusted feed.
  3. Prefer reviewed or practical entries.
  4. Use Method and Lens metadata to refine the approach, not to inflate evidence.
  5. Show the action, evidence state, Brali page, and direct sources for reviewed research-derived material.
  6. When provenance matters, inspect the associated Evidence Decision rather than inferring rationale from the final protocol alone.

For improving Brali

Use a different path: research candidate → evidence decision → hack → protocol → ontology → publish. Search output is deliberately not publishable evidence.

  1. Use Ontology Coverage to choose a real taxonomy or research gap.
  2. Load Research Discovery and add deduplicated leads with canonical Domain/Topic/Method/Lens IDs.
  3. Load Evidence Review, read the actual source, and record a decision with supported claim, unsupported claims, and limitations.
  4. A watch decision may inform taxonomy or future research but must not be converted into a recommendation.
  5. Use Protocol Design only after a decision supports an actionable proposal.
  6. Use Taxonomy Curation without breaking legacy URLs.

Machine-readable files

  • ontology.json — Domains, Topics, Methods, Lenses, and legacy mapping.
  • ontology-coverage.json — migration and growth-gap coverage.
  • protocols.json — trusted protocol feed schema v3.
  • hacks.json — broader library corpus.
  • evidence.json — public evidence status index.
  • evidence-decisions.json — reviewed source-to-editorial-decision audit trail.
  • review-queue.json — evidence review queue.
  • research-queries.json — maintained ontology-mapped research lenses.
  • research-candidates.json — discovery records; workflow status is not itself evidence.
  • agents/registry.json — agent roles and skill paths.
  • llms.txt — project guide for language-model tools.

Evidence boundary

A source being present is not the same as a source being reviewed. A DOI, title, abstract, citation count, search rank, or AI summary cannot promote an item to reviewed. A candidate with status watch is still not a recommendation.

Do not erase uncertainty

Keep the evidence state, decision, source scope, ontology classification status, and important limitations when reusing Brali content. Do not turn an association into causation, a Method into a Topic, or a narrow study into a universal rule.

MCP

The JSON files and contracts are the public interface today. A future MCP service can add search, read, recommendation, evidence-decision, coverage, and ontology tools over the same canonical data.
