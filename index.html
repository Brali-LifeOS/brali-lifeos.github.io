Evidence matters

We do not pretend every useful idea is proven science.

Brali keeps practical suggestions separate from stronger evidence-based claims. Reviewed items have a higher source bar. Weak or sensitive material can stay out of normal discovery until it is checked.

The original app

The library is now the main project.

Brali began as a large habit tracker and personal organizer with routines, journals, planning, challenges, metrics, and more. The app is still useful, but the long-term focus is the knowledge itself: better structure, better evidence, more languages, and easier access for people and AI.
