Evidence matters

We do not pretend every useful idea is proven science.

Reviewed material has a higher source bar. Practical suggestions stay separate from evidence claims, and weaker or sensitive records can stay out of normal discovery until checked.

The original app

The library is now the main project.

Brali began as a large habit tracker and personal organizer. The long-term focus is now the knowledge itself: clearer structure, stronger evidence, reproducible data, multilingual identity, and easier access for people and AI systems.
