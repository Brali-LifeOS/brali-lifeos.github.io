Evidence without theatre

We show what is known, what is practical, and what still needs review.

Reviewed protocol records keep explicit evidence states, source provenance, and stable identities attached to the action. Versioned datasets and reproducible retrieval artifacts preserve that context when the same knowledge is reused.

Trust is part of the content.

Reviewed, practical, pending-review, and restricted states change what belongs in normal discovery. Uncertainty is not hidden behind a confidence score.
