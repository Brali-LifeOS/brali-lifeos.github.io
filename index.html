Choose Up to Three Priority Outcomes for the Day

PracticalEvidence state

Choose a small set of outcomes that deserves protected attention today, define what done means, and move the rest to a visible backlog instead of pretending every task is equally urgent.

Steps and evidence
TryReview today's deadlines, dependencies, and available capacity; choose up to three outcomes that most deserve progress; define what done means for each; reserve appropriate time where possible; place other tasks in a visible backlog; and re-plan explicitly when genuinely urgent work changes the day.Now
