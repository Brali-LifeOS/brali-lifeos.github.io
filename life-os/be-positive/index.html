  • Pause Long Enough to Notice a Good MomentChoose a positive experience that is already real: a good meal, a joke, a quiet walk, finishing useful work, music, a kind message, or another ordinary moment. For a short period, stop adding new input. Notice one or two concrete details and what makes the moment worth noticing. Then continue your day. Do not use the exercise to deny an unpleasant feeling or to manufacture gratitude for something that is not good.reviewed
