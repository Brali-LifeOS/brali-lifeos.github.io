Build everyday wellbeing with small practices, without forcing positivity or pretending every setback needs a silver lining.

Do It

Reduce the friction between intending and starting: clearer next actions, better cues, and fewer productivity rituals.

Skill Sprint

Learn by practising the part that matters: retrieval, feedback, application, and review.

Be Healthy

Everyday health and movement practices with clear evidence and safety boundaries — not diagnosis in a nicer font.

Phrases

Useful words for boundaries, persistence, and difficult moments — chosen for the situation, not pasted in as motivation wallpaper.

Be Creative

Generate more varied ideas, change context when it helps, and separate idea generation from evaluation.

Be Calm

Low-risk ways to work with everyday stress. Calm is a useful state, not an on-demand performance metric.

Mood & Low Mood

General wellbeing practices for mood and daily functioning, with sensitive guidance kept separate from professional mental-health care.

Fear & Confidence

Practical ways to understand fear, approach manageable challenges, and build confidence without promising fearlessness.

Stoicism

Use Stoic ideas as a lens for control, perspective, and action — philosophy first, motivational mythology second.

Money

Small experiments for spending, saving, planning, and financial decision hygiene — not individualized financial advice.

Antifragility

Use antifragility as a strategic lens for optionality, downside limits, and learning from manageable stressors.

Language

Practise vocabulary, retrieval, feedback, and real use instead of collecting language-learning tricks.

Future Builder

Turn a future goal into assumptions, controllable next moves, and review points you can actually revisit.

Talk Smart

Make communication clearer: listen accurately, choose the right medium, rehearse hard conversations, and repair misunderstandings.

Relationships

Build better conversations, boundaries, repair, and shared routines without pretending one script fixes every relationship.

Avoid Errors

Reduce avoidable mistakes with checks, constraints, review points, and less faith in memory at the worst possible moment.

Fit Life

Make movement easier to repeat with small, measurable routines and realistic evidence boundaries.

TRIZ

Use TRIZ patterns to reframe constraints and generate alternatives when ordinary brainstorming is going in circles.

Data

Use simple measurement, uncertainty, and comparison to make decisions without pretending every number deserves a dashboard.

As QA

Borrow QA habits for everyday work: define failure modes, make rejection cheap, and test before trusting.

Marketing

Clarify audience, value, message, proof, and distribution without turning ordinary communication into growth-hacking theatre.

Heart Health Basics

General educational material about everyday cardiovascular-health habits; not diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for medical care.

Insider

Practical shortcuts and context that are useful because they remove friction, not because they sound secret.

As Architector

Design environments, information flows, and constraints so the system helps instead of requiring daily heroics.

Chef

Borrow mise-en-place thinking: prepare inputs, sequence work, reduce switching, and clean the system as you go.

As Detective

Separate observations from stories, test competing explanations, and look for the clue that would change your mind.

Quantum

Explore real quantum ideas without turning physics into life-coaching metaphors.

Work

Design clearer work systems, decisions, handoffs, and reviews instead of collecting productivity rituals.

NLP

Explore NLP-inspired communication ideas critically and treat them as heuristics, not established psychological mechanisms.

Thinking

Frame problems, separate claims from evidence, generate alternatives, and build better checks around judgment.

Writing

Plan, draft, revise, and get useful feedback without turning every sentence into a content strategy.

Grow fast

Run small growth experiments, review the signal, and keep what works instead of chasing transformation streaks.

Grandmaster

Borrow chess habits for planning, trade-offs, tempo, and looking one move beyond the obvious reply.

Game Theory

Model incentives, alternatives, repeated interactions, and what the other side is likely to do next.

Psychodynamic

Psychodynamic-inspired reflection prompts for patterns and past influences, kept separate from clinical treatment claims.

Metacognitive

Metacognitive-inspired ways to notice how attention relates to worry and repetitive thinking, with clinical claims review-gated.

Cognitive Analytic

Cognitive-analytic-inspired reflection on repeating patterns and relationships, without presenting self-help prompts as therapy.

Positive Psychotherapy

Positive-psychology-inspired practices around strengths and positive experiences, kept separate from clinical treatment claims.

Body-Oriented

Body-awareness practices for noticing tension and physical experience, with health and treatment claims kept review-gated.

Ericksonian

Explore story, reframing, and suggestion as communication ideas without treating them as a substitute for professional therapy.

Gestalt

Gestalt-inspired awareness and reflection practices, presented as educational prompts rather than clinical treatment.

Exposure

Evidence-aware education about approaching manageable fears gradually, with safety and clinical boundaries made explicit.

DBT

DBT-inspired educational skills for everyday coping and communication, kept separate from diagnosis and treatment.

ACT

ACT-inspired practices for values, acceptance, and useful action, with clinical treatment claims kept review-gated.

CBT

CBT-inspired educational practices for examining thoughts and testing useful actions, not self-administered diagnosis or treatment.

Grow Together

Practical relationship routines for shared goals, repair, and cooperation without pretending one script fixes every conflict.

Cognitive Biases

Recognize predictable reasoning shortcuts and design checks that make the mistake harder to repeat.

Stratagema

Use classic stratagems as a lens for constraints, incentives, and asymmetric moves — not as a permission slip for manipulation.

Using AI

Use AI where it earns its place: draft, explore, transform, and check — while keeping verification and the final decision with a human.
