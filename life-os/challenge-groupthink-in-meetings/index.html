In Group Discussions: - Ask Questions:

Protocol summary · practical

Try this

In group discussions: - Ask questions: "Have we considered other perspectives?" - Encourage dissent: Create a safe space for alternative viewpoints. - Pause before deciding: Suggest taking time to explore overlooked options. Example: Before agreeing with a team’s rushed decision, propose reviewing potential risks and alternatives.

Check-in: Check in when you challenge groupthink and push for diverse perspectives.
