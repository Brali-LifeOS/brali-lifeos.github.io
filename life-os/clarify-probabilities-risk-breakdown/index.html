Clarify Probabilities

Protocol summary · practical

Try this

To improve judgment: - Break it down: Divide events into clear, mutually exclusive parts. - Add them up: Compare individual probabilities to the total likelihood. - Check your math: Avoid underestimating by verifying if the sum makes sense. Example: When assessing risks for a project, split them into categories (e.g., budget, time, resources) and evaluate each separately.

Check-in: Check in when you clarify probabilities for better decision-making.
