  • Take a Short Walk When Stress BuildsWhen you notice stress building, consider taking a short walk if walking is safe and practical for you. Treat ten minutes as a convenient boundary, not a medically proven dose. Walk at a comfortable pace in a safe place, then notice whether you feel any different before returning to the next task.reviewed
  • Try Progressive Muscle Relaxation GentlySit or lie somewhere comfortable and work through a few muscle groups one at a time. Gently tense each group for no more than about five seconds, then release it and notice the contrast. Do not strain, and skip or release any area that causes significant discomfort.reviewed
