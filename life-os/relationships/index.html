  • Make a Specific Repair Attempt After a Workable ConflictWait until you can speak without escalating. Name the specific action or words you are taking responsibility for. Acknowledge the impact as you understand it without using your intention to cancel the other person's experience. Apologize for your part without an 'if'. Offer one concrete repair or behavior change you can actually make, then ask how the other person sees it. Listen to the answer. Do not require forgiveness, agreement, or immediate trust in return.reviewed
