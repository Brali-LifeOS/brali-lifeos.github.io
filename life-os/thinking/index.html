These entries currently meet Brali's reviewed/practical retrieval bar. “Trusted” is a publication state, not a promise that every protocol has equal scientific support.

  • AI-Assisted PremortemDescribe the plan without sensitive information, ask an AI for plausible ways it could fail, group the suggestions, verify the important ones yourself, and choose mitigations only for risks that survive the check.practical
  • Ask for Feedback on One Specific DecisionState the intended outcome and the current decision. Ask one focused question such as what is unclear, what assumption looks wrong or what should change first. Choose a reviewer who can judge that question, then record what you will accept, test, discuss, defer or ignore.practical
  • Judge a Commitment by What Comes NextName the decision. Separate unrecoverable past cost from future cost. List the realistic benefit of continuing, the best alternative use of the same resources, and any obligation or switching cost that genuinely exists now. Then choose stop, continue, change, or gather one missing fact.practical
  • Record What Would Change Your Confidence Before You CommitState the decision or prediction, label your confidence in ordinary language, list the strongest support and the most important unknown, name one observable condition that would make you update, then revisit the record when the outcome or new information becomes available.practical
  • Run a Missing-Evidence Check Before a DecisionWrite the example or story currently driving the decision. Ask whether it is representative or simply vivid, recent, or personal. Look for one counterexample and, when the decision warrants it, one relevant denominator, base rate, or source. Update the decision only as far as the new information supports.practical
  • Run One Disconfirming Check Before a Consequential CommitmentWrite the assumption driving the decision, state one observation that would materially weaken it, identify where that information can be checked, inspect it before the commitment becomes difficult to reverse, and then keep, narrow, change or escalate the plan according to what you found.practical
