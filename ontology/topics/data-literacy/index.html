Data Literacy

Use data, measurement, uncertainty, and simple analysis to make better judgments.

  • Data Analysts Automate Routine ReportsData analysts automate routine reports. Use automation tools to generate regular reports on your progress, goals, or any other relevant data.
  • Check Whether a Streak Changes the Next EventBefore acting on a streak, identify whether earlier outcomes can change the next event and use a pre-defined decision rule rather than a story about what is due.
  • Write the Inclusion Rule Before GeneralizingRecord who or what entered an analysis, who was excluded and how the conclusion changes when missing cases are considered.
  • Data Analysts Keep up with Industry Trends and ToolsData analysts keep up with industry trends and tools. Regularly read articles, attend webinars, and join professional groups to stay updated with the latest trends in your field.
  • Data Analysts Ensure Data Accuracy by Cleaning ItData analysts ensure data accuracy by cleaning it. Regularly review and update your records, schedules, and plans to keep them accurate and relevant.
  • Data Analysts Present Their Findings ClearlyData analysts present their findings clearly. Practice summarizing and presenting your data or results in a clear and concise manner.
  • Data Analysts Use Statistical Tools to Interpret DataData analysts use statistical tools to interpret data. Learn to use basic statistical tools or software to analyze your personal or professional data.
  • Data Analysts Focus on Key Performance Indicators (kpis)Data analysts focus on key performance indicators (KPIs). Determine the most important metrics in your life or work that align with your goals.
  • Data Analysts Use Predictive Analysis to Forecast Future TrendsData analysts use predictive analysis to forecast future trends. Apply predictive analysis techniques to anticipate future outcomes in your projects or personal goals.
  • Data Analysts Identify Patterns and Trends in DataData analysts identify patterns and trends in data. Regularly review your personal or professional activities to spot trends and make informed decisions.
  • Data Analysts Test Hypotheses to Validate AssumptionsData analysts test hypotheses to validate assumptions. Formulate hypotheses about your work or personal life and test them to see if they hold true.
  • Data Analysts Use Charts and Graphs to Make Data UnderstandableData analysts use charts and graphs to make data understandable. Create visual representations of your goals, progress, or any data relevant to you.
