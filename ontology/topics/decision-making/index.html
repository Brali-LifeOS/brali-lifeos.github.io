Decision Making

Structure choices under uncertainty and update decisions when evidence changes.

Status: growth-gap. 6 current library entries map here through legacy or reviewed record-level classification.

  • AI-Assisted PremortemUse an AI model as a brainstorming partner for possible failure modes, then verify important risks against the real plan before choosing mitigations.
  • Judge Each Option Against the Same CriteriaDefine the decision criteria before comparing options, assess each option on its own and only then compare the results.
  • Check Whether a Streak Changes the Next EventBefore acting on a streak, identify whether earlier outcomes can change the next event and use a pre-defined decision rule rather than a story about what is due.
  • Draw Two Circles; One for Factors You Can Control and Another for Those YouDraw two circles; one for factors you can control and another for those you cannot. Set clear, achievable goals based on what’s within your control.
  • Decision Calibration JournalBefore an uncertain decision, record your expectation, confidence, and key assumption. Later compare them with the outcome and update how you make similar choices.
  • When Feeling Overwhelmed or Upset, Pause and Take Three Deep Breaths Before ReactingWhen feeling overwhelmed or upset, pause and take three deep breaths before reacting.
