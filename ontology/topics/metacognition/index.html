Metacognition

Think about how you learn, reason, monitor confidence, and adjust strategy.

Status: growth-gap. 4 current library entries map here through legacy or reviewed record-level classification.

  • Ask for Feedback on One Specific DecisionGive the reviewer context, ask one focused question, make the reply easy and decide what you will accept, test, discuss, defer or ignore.
  • Record What Would Change a BeliefWrite a claim, the basis for your current view, the new information and the condition under which you would revise, narrow or suspend the belief.
  • Decision Calibration JournalBefore an uncertain decision, record your expectation, confidence, and key assumption. Later compare them with the outcome and update how you make similar choices.
  • Deliberate Attention Switching with Everyday SoundsUse several neutral sounds around you to practice deliberately selecting one sound, switching to another, and then noticing several at once. Treat this as an attention-flexibility
