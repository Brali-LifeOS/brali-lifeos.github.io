Ways to work together

  • Commercial dataset licensing: use Brali data in a paid product or service.
  • AI integrations: connect agents and recommendation systems to Brali data.
  • Localization: create reviewed editions for new languages and markets.
  • Custom collections: build a focused set for learning, work, wellbeing, decision-making, or another clear use case.
  • Research and editorial work: improve evidence, taxonomy, sources, and content quality.

Why partner with Brali?

The value is not just a large list of tips. Brali connects each useful idea to a category, action, evidence state, and source page. That makes the library easier to search, review, translate, and reuse.

What stays public?

The public library can remain useful for individuals, researchers, and non-commercial projects. Commercial work can fund the slower parts: review, maintenance, localization, and reliable integrations.

Commercial dataset licensing

The current public license is non-commercial. A separate agreement can cover paid products, commercial AI use, redistribution, or custom data delivery.

AI and agent integrations

Brali already publishes JSON feeds. A future MCP service may make search and retrieval easier, but partners do not need to wait for it to explore the data model.

Principles

  • Keep the evidence state visible.
  • Keep source links and protocol identity where possible.
  • Keep translations connected to the original protocol.
  • Do not use the Brali name or logo as part of another product without permission.
  • Use a higher review bar for sensitive topics.

Start a conversation

For a public proposal, open an issue in the GitHub repository. Do not post confidential information there. A private commercial contact route will be added before paid services launch.
