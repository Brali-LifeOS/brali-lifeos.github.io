Research collaborations

Turn a useful question into transparent, bounded knowledge.

Brali welcomes collaborations that improve evidence quality, knowledge structure, retrieval, or practical interpretation without overstating what a source can support.

Evidence

Review a claim boundary

Trace a practical claim to the underlying source, population, outcome, and uncertainty.

Structure

Improve the ontology

Clarify Domains, Topics, aliases, and relationships without breaking canonical identity.

Evaluation

Test retrieval quality

Study whether people or agents receive relevant protocols with evidence and provenance intact.

Research principles

  • Define the question, method, sources, and expected output before promotion into the library.
  • Record weak, null, negative, and contradictory findings instead of filtering for a preferred answer.
  • Keep discovery leads separate from reviewed evidence decisions.
  • State population, setting, outcome, time horizon, and important limitations when they affect interpretation.
  • Do not put confidential, personal, participant, or client-restricted information in a public GitHub issue.

Before proposing

Review the current research surface, content methodology, and source policy. A useful proposal names the gap it addresses and the public artifact it can leave behind.

Propose a research collaboration

Describe the question, proposed method, sources or deliverables, and what you or your organization can contribute.
