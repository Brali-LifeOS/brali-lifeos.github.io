Decision rule

A source can support one behavior, outcome, population, or setting without validating the complete Protocol. When the exact schedule, wrapper, broad outcome, or universal applicability is not supported, Brali rewrites the public action and keeps the record practical.

Use a Focus Block and a Short Break

Source: Albulescu et al. (2022), systematic review and meta-analysis of micro-breaks. DOI

Supports: short breaks can support momentary well-being, particularly vigor and fatigue, across the synthesized studies.

Does not support: a universal twenty-five-minute schedule, a general performance increase, or inherited exact outcome claims.

Public change: flexible work block, real break, fatigue and restart check.
