Which learning practices improve durable memory, and how should Brali separate retention from transfer and application?

Prefer meta-analyses and classroom/field studies that distinguish direct retention, forward effects, and application outcomes.

Resolved 2026-08-20. Memory moved from an ontology growth gap to active coverage after Brali reviewed current evidence on wakeful rest and retrieval practice, narrowed unsupported transfer claims, and published trusted protocols that preserve those evidence boundaries.

Evidence boundary

Do not collapse remembering, learning later material, and skill application into one testing-effect claim.
