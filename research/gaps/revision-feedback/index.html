Research Gap · Creativity & Expression · Resolved

Which forms of feedback and revision improve written or creative work, and when can feedback backfire?

Prefer systematic reviews and comparative studies with revision-quality or learning outcomes.

Resolved 2026-08-21. Revision & Feedback moved from a growth gap to active coverage after Brali reviewed a 2024 meta-analysis of educational writing feedback, replaced inherited unsupported peer-review claims with a bounded protocol that distinguishes surface and deep revision targets, mapped that protocol to the canonical Topic, and preserved explicit limits on learner population, feedback source, sequence, and transfer beyond educational writing.

Evidence boundary

More feedback is not automatically better; timing, source, specificity, and task matter.
