Living research pipeline

Brali now keeps the research process as data instead of hiding it inside one prompt.

  1. Search lenses: research-queries.json maps maintained scholarly queries to Life Areas and Growth Zones.
  2. Discovery queue: the weekly Research Scout deduplicates recent scholarly metadata into research-candidates.json.
  3. Evidence decision: an Evidence Reviewer reads the actual source and records the narrow claim it supports, challenges, or fails to support.
  4. Library change: only then can a candidate strengthen an existing hack, propose a new atomic hack, or become part of an executable protocol.

Why the queue is deliberately untrusted

Search metadata is useful for discovery, not for conclusions. A title can sound relevant while the population, intervention, outcome, effect size, or limitations say something much narrower. Candidate records therefore cannot receive Brali's reviewed evidence state.

What belongs here

  • Evidence notes: check an idea against research and explain what the sources do and do not support.
  • Trend notes: watch useful developments in behavior change, learning, wellbeing, productivity, and AI-assisted tools.
  • Open questions: record questions that could improve the library, recommendations, evidence model, protocols, or translations.
  • Contradictions: record good evidence that challenges a popular hack instead of quietly ignoring it.

Current questions

  • Which small practices are most useful in everyday life?
  • Which old Brali hacks should be removed, narrowed, or merged?
  • Which hacks combine naturally into protocols rather than separate articles?
  • How can Brali show evidence without making the library hard to read?
  • How can an AI agent recommend a protocol without overstating confidence?
  • Which therapy-named zones are really methods/tags rather than user destinations?
  • How should one hack or protocol stay consistent across several languages?

From research to the library

A research note or candidate does not automatically become a trusted protocol. It can improve an existing hack, add or remove a claim, change an evidence state after review, create a new atomic hack proposal, or suggest a protocol composed from existing actions.
