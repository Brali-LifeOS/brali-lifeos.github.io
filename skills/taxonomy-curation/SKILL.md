# Brali Taxonomy Curation

Read `data/life-areas.json`, `data/life-os-zones.json`, and the candidate hack/protocol.

Choose the smallest set of Life Areas and Growth Zones that matches the user's actual need. Suggest method tags separately.

Before adding a zone, check whether the idea is a method, a synonym, or a narrow subtopic that should remain a tag.

Never change an existing canonical slug as a side effect of classification. Produce migration proposals separately.

Flag duplicate or ambiguous zones and explain the preferred mapping.
