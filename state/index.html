How to read these numbers

Reviewed and practical are Brali publication states. They describe editorial trust handling, not one universal level of scientific proof. Topic coverage measures taxonomy mapping. Source coverage measures whether a usable source is recorded in the evidence index.

What changes over time

The Evidence Trends report tracks review activity and the evidence-state distribution. Brali Updates covers broader project changes, while Research & trends explains individual evidence corrections and new findings.
