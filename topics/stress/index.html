Protocols to try

7 trusted protocol recommendations available.

Flagship 100 · reviewed

Pause Before Acting on a High-Stress Urge

Pause. Describe the situation in one factual sentence and name the action urge without judging it. Choose a harmless brief activity that creates time rather than escalating the situation. Then reassess what action is safest and most useful.

Check-in: Did you create a pause without making the situation worse, and what safe next action did you choose? Do not score success by whether the emotion disappeared.

Flagship 100 · reviewed

Use Your Senses to Return to the Present

Pause somewhere safe and deliberately notice what is happening around you right now. Choose a few sights, sounds, textures, smells or tastes and describe them simply. When your attention drifts into planning, worry or commentary, notice that and gently return to one sensory detail.

Check-in: What did I notice, how easy or difficult was it to return attention to the present, and did this feel useful enough to try again?

Flagship 100 · reviewed

Action and Mood Observation Note

Write the situation, the activity you chose, how you felt before, and how you felt afterward. Add one alternative explanation so the note does not turn correlation into certainty.

Check-in: What did you observe, and what would be worth trying or discussing next without assuming the activity caused the change?

Flagship 100 · reviewed

Comfortable Diaphragmatic Breathing

Sit or lie in a comfortable position, let the breath slow without forcing a count, notice the abdomen rise and fall, and stop after a few easy cycles or whenever you prefer.

Check-in: Did the exercise feel comfortable and leave you ready for the next ordinary action? Stop if you feel dizzy, short of breath, distressed, or otherwise unwell.

Flagship 100 · practical

Draw Two Circles; One for Factors You Can Control and Another for Those You

Write down one concern, separate the outcome from the actions you can take, convert anything you can influence into one concrete behavior, and choose the next useful action without pretending you control the final result.

Check-in: What part of this situation was actually mine to act on, what remained outside my control, and did the sorting help me choose a clearer next step?

Flagship 100 · practical

Comfortable Breathing Reset

Sit or stand comfortably, let your breathing slow without forcing a count, take a few easy breaths, and then return attention to one concrete next action.

Check-in: Did the pause help you feel more settled and able to choose the next action? Stop if breathing feels uncomfortable or light-headed.

Flagship 100 · practical

Short Resilience Reminder

Write one sentence in the form: This is difficult, and the next thing I can control is ___. Keep it specific to the current situation.

Check-in: Did the phrase lead to a concrete action, or did it become empty motivation that you repeated without changing anything?
